# Sample Homeowners Policy — Synthetic Training Document

This document is synthetic and is provided only for software development.

## Section 1 — Water Damage

The policy covers sudden and accidental water damage caused by a burst interior
supply pipe, subject to the deductible and all other policy conditions.

The policy does not cover damage caused by repeated seepage or leakage that
occurs over a period of fourteen days or more when the insured knew, or should
reasonably have known, about the condition.

## Section 2 — Emergency Mitigation

The insured must take reasonable steps to protect the property from further
damage. Reasonable emergency mitigation expenses may be reimbursed when they
are necessary and properly documented.

## Section 3 — Claim Documentation

The insured should provide photographs of the damage, repair estimates,
receipts for emergency mitigation, and a description of when and how the loss
was discovered.

## Section 4 — Coverage Decisions

Only an authorized claims representative may issue a final coverage decision.
Information produced by an automated assistant is not a binding coverage
determination.
